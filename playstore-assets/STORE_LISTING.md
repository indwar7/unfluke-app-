## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Unfluke is a powerful trading strategy platform built for serious traders in Indian and crypto markets. Backtest, simulate, and validate your strategies before risking real capital.

KEY FEATURES

• Strategy Backtester — Test your trading strategies against years of historical data with the Basic and Advanced Backtester
• Option Simulator — Simulate option strategies with real expiry data, IV, lot sizes, and live spot/futures pricing for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and stocks
• Pre-built Strategies — Bullish, Bearish, and Non-Directional templates: Long Call, Short Put, Bull Call Spread, Iron Condor, and more
• Advanced Scanner — Filter stocks by technical indicators, fundamentals, and custom screeners
• Strategy Charts — Visualize payoff diagrams, Greeks, and P&L curves
• Historical Data — Minute-level historical data for accurate backtesting
• Telegram Alerts — Get strategy alerts directly to your Telegram
• Fundamental Analysis — DuPont, key financials, valuation ratios for listed companies
• Leaderboard & Top Performers — Track top-performing strategies and users
• Notifications — Real-time alerts for backtest results and signals

WHO IS UNFLUKE FOR?

• Options traders who want to validate strategies before going live
• Swing & positional traders building rule-based systems
• Algo traders prototyping ideas without writing code
• Anyone learning to trade the right way — with data, not guesswork

DISCLAIMER

Unfluke is a trading analysis and simulation tool. We do not provide investment advice, stock tips, or guaranteed returns. Trading in financial markets involves risk; users should make decisions based on their own research and risk tolerance. Past performance shown in backtests does not guarantee future results.
