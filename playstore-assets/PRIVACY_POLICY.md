## 1. About Unfluke

Unfluke is a trading analysis and education platform that helps users:
- Backtest trading strategies on historical Indian equity, F&O, and crypto market data
- Run option strategy simulations and scenario analysis
- Access market scanners and technical analysis tools
- Learn quantitative trading methods

**Important:** Unfluke is **not** a stockbroker, investment advisor, or research analyst. We do **not** execute trades, do **not** integrate with any broker, do **not** route orders, and do **not** provide buy/sell recommendations. The App does **not** facilitate any live trading activity. All content is for educational, analytical, and informational purposes only. Because Unfluke does not undertake any regulated activity (such as advisory, brokerage, or portfolio management), SEBI registration is **not applicable**.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account information:** name, email address, mobile phone number, password (stored as bcrypt hash)
- **Profile data:** trading experience level, preferences, referral code
- **Communications:** messages, feedback, and support queries you send us
- **User-generated content:** saved strategies, custom scanners, notes
- **Payment information:** handled by our payment processors (CCAvenue/HDFC Payment Gateway); we receive only transaction status and metadata — **we do not store your full card number, CVV, or net banking credentials**
- **Optional integrations:** Telegram username (if you choose to receive alerts via Telegram)

### 2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do **not** collect:
- PAN, Aadhaar, or other government identifiers
- Bank account numbers or net banking credentials
- Broker account credentials, API keys, or trading account passwords
- Demat account details
- KYC documents

Unfluke does **not** integrate with stockbrokers and does **not** require access to your trading or demat accounts.

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device information:** device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, app version, language settings
- **Usage data:** features used, screens visited, time spent, in-app actions, click patterns
- **Diagnostics & crash logs:** stack traces, error reports, performance metrics (via Firebase Crashlytics)
- **Network information:** IP address, approximate geographic location based on IP (city/region level only — we do **not** collect GPS or precise location)
- **Cookies and similar technologies** (on the Website only): session cookies, analytics cookies

### 2.4 Information from Third Parties

- **Firebase Authentication:** when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google
- **OTP gateway (WhatsApp/SMS):** delivery status of verification messages
- **Analytics providers:** aggregated usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics)

### 2.5 Permissions Used by the Mobile App

The App may request the following permissions:
- **Internet:** required to load data and connect to our servers
- **Notifications:** to send you alerts about backtests, scanner results, and account updates (you can disable this in device settings)
- **Storage (optional):** to save exported reports or charts
- **Camera (optional):** only if you upload a profile picture

The App does **not** access your contacts, calendar, microphone, SMS messages, call logs, or precise location.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Storage Location

Your data is primarily stored on servers located in **India** (AWS Mumbai / Google Cloud Mumbai region). Some data may be processed on servers outside India for technical reasons (e.g., global CDN, analytics).

### 5.2 Security Measures

- All data in transit is encrypted using **HTTPS/TLS 1.2+**
- Passwords stored using **bcrypt hashing**
- Sensitive data encrypted at rest (AES-256)
- Role-based access control for internal systems
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
- Incident response plan in place

While we follow industry best practices, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India within the timeframes required under the DPDP Act.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

- **Active account data:** retained while your account is active
- **Inactive accounts:** deleted automatically after 24 months of inactivity (with prior notice)
- **Transaction records:** retained for 8 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws
- **Communications and support tickets:** retained for 3 years
- **Backups:** rotated and purged within 90 days

You may request earlier deletion (see Section 7).

## 7. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

- **Right to access:** request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to correction:** correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to erasure:** request deletion of your account and personal data
- **Right to grievance redressal:** raise complaints with our Grievance Officer (Section 13)
- **Right to nominate:** nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity
- **Right to withdraw consent:** withdraw consent for processing at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing)
- **Right to data portability:** receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email **support@unfluke.in** with your request. We will respond within **30 days**.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account in two ways:

1. **In-app:** Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account
2. **Email:** Send a request to **support@unfluke.in** with subject "Delete my account"
